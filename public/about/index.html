About Vent Viper

Vent Viper is owned and operated by Danny Dooner, right here in Wyckoff, NJ.

Danny started Vent Viper with a simple idea: homeowners in Bergen County deserve a reliable local pro they can actually talk to — not a franchise call center, not a random contractor from three counties away. When you call Vent Viper, Danny picks up the phone. When he shows up at your door, he’s your neighbor.

Five Trades, One Operator

Vent Viper covers five precision home services, every one of them performed by Danny himself:

  • Dryer Vent Cleaning — the original. Clogged dryer vents are one of the leading causes of house fires in the U.S. Danny cleans the entire path from your dryer to the exterior cap and shows you exactly what was trapped inside, so you can see the hazard for yourself.
  • Air Duct Cleaning — whole-home HVAC and central-air ductwork. Cleaner ducts mean better air quality, more efficient cooling and heating, and longer equipment life.
  • Gutter Cleaning & Installation — hand-clearing, downspout flushing, and full seamless aluminum gutter installs with leaf-guard options. Stops ice dams, foundation rot, and overflow damage.
  • Pressure Washing — driveways, walkways, patios, decks, siding, fences. Soft-wash on delicate surfaces, full-pressure on hardscape.
  • Pest Control — targeted residential treatment for ants, spiders, mice, wasps, ticks, and mosquitoes. Quarterly programs available. Family- and pet-conscious products.

The Neighborhood Drop — Group Rates
