About Vent Viper

Vent Viper is owned and operated by Danny Dooner, right here in Wyckoff, NJ.

Danny started Vent Viper with a simple idea: homeowners in Bergen County deserve a reliable local pro they can actually talk to — not a franchise call center, not a random contractor from three counties away. When you call Vent Viper, Danny picks up the phone. When he shows up at your door, he’s your neighbor.

What We Do

Dryer vent cleaning is the core of what Vent Viper does — and it’s more important than most homeowners realize. Clogged dryer vents are one of the leading causes of house fires in the U.S. Danny cleans the entire path from your dryer to the exterior exhaust and shows you exactly what was trapped inside so you can see the hazard for yourself.

Beyond vents, Danny handles air duct maintenance, gutter cleaning, and snow removal for residential customers across Wyckoff, Franklin Lakes, Ridgewood, Glen Rock, Midland Park, and the surrounding Bergen County area.

Why Local Matters

Danny doesn’t try to cover all of New Jersey. He stays focused on the neighborhoods he knows — the Wyckoff and Franklin Lakes corridor and surrounding towns. That means faster response times, personal attention, and a guy who actually cares about doing the job right because his reputation in the community depends on it.

You’ll find Danny recommended in the Wyckoff Facebook groups, on Nextdoor, and by word-of-mouth from your actual neighbors. That kind of trust doesn’t come from a marketing budget — it comes from showing up, doing great work, and being easy to deal with.
